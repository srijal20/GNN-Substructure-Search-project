1. Title of the Project

GNN Molecular Substructure Search: Accuracy vs. Speed Trade-offs Against Classical Fingerprint Screening

2. Updates on Problem Statement & Cascaded Framework

Exact subgraph isomorphism matching (Q ⊆ T) via combinatorial algorithms (VF2/Ullmann in RDKit) scales exponentially in the worst case O(|VT||VQ|) and becomes computationally prohibitive across large chemical databases (50,000+ molecules). Because pure neural classifiers suffer from false negatives that can discard critical bioactive scaffolds, we have formally refined our pipeline into a two-stage cascaded filtering paradigm: (1) A bond-aware Graph Isomorphism Network with NeuroMatch-style order embeddings (zQ ≤ zT element-wise in ℝ≥064) screens the database in <3 ms on GPU to shortlist candidates at a strict high-recall constraint (≥98%), and (2) RDKit's exact HasSubstructMatch is evaluated only on the shortlisted pool, guaranteeing 100% precision. The objective is quantifying the Pareto frontier of this neural cascade against the industrial gold standard: RDKit's 2048-bit Chem.PatternFingerprint bitwise screen followed by exact verification.

  • Shortlist Overhead & Verification Bottleneck: GNN B is over 2× faster at vector screening than the fingerprint screen (2.79 ms vs. 1.09 ms). However, at the ≥98% recall threshold, its false positive rate produces ~1,414 candidates (vs. 703 for Pattern FP). At ~0.05 ms per RDKit check, verifying false positives creates an execution bottleneck, lowering cascaded speedup from a theoretical 11× to 1.6×.
  • Query-Size Receptive Field Decay (4 vs. 12 Atoms): Empirical breakdown shows strong recall on small fragments (4 atoms: 83.4%; 5 atoms: 83.7%), but sharp degradation on larger subgraphs (9 atoms: 67.8%; 10 atoms: 28.4%; 12 atoms: 43.6%). The 4-layer GINEConv receptive field and global sum-pooling struggle to distinguish long-range cycle topologies as molecular diameter expands.
  • CPU Bottleneck in Hard-Negative Mining: Constructing balanced training pairs requiring hard negatives (pairs passing bitwise fingerprint screens but failing exact topological matching) requires >40,000 CPU-bound RDKit exact calls during batching.
